Our story

From a 3-person studio to a global product squad.

ZynTechX started in 2014 with a single belief: senior, accountable teams ship better software than offshore body-shops ever will. Twelve years and 200+ launches later, we still operate that way — small, embedded squads with skin in the game.

Today our team of 48 spans Karachi, Dubai and London, partnering with venture-backed founders and Fortune-500 product orgs alike.

Our Mission

Empower ambitious teams with software that compounds — fast to ship, easy to evolve, hard to break.
